# Antonio Wolf corpus: reading coverage

23 September 2026. **Full online corpus reading remains incomplete.**

The public WordPress API returned 175 posts and 13 pages, totaling approximately 479,198 extracted words before deduplication. This includes translations, notices, navigation pages, and video/link-only entries; it is not 188 original philosophical essays.

**Read in full: 51 WordPress articles plus 2 Epoché essays, 53 pieces total.** The WordPress full-text total is 144,788 extracted words, approximately 30.2% of the retrieved WordPress word count. This turn added 28 full articles / 93,116 words, through 41 reading chunks. The two earlier translation articles and one additional Epoché article remain conservatively marked passage-reviewed.

The user requested “all you can digest.” The second pass therefore extended beyond language-design keywords into recognition, aesthetics, art, games, and the author's self-criticism. It did not complete the online corpus. Older publication dates may conceal extensive 2026 revisions; both publication and modification dates remain in the JSON inventory. Author claims are evidence of the author's interpretation, not independently established facts about every discipline discussed.

The linked YouTube channel supplied no readable recording content. Attempts to open the Upheaval video and the Preface lecture playlist failed, and a general transcript search did not establish a usable transcript. No recording is counted as watched or read. Social accounts, other blogs, comments, deleted pages, and unlisted writings are not exhaustively inventoried. The Epoché author index lists more work than has been read.
