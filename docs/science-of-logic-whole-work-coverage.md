# Science of Logic: whole-work reading inventory

23 September 2026. **Whole work: incomplete.** This inventory is separate from the Antonio Wolf inventory. It records a first primary-text reading, not exhaustive interpretation or comparison of editions.

Collected: **57 HTML pages**, **333,310 extracted words**, including headings and navigation. Examined in full in this continuation: **19 pages**, **94,821 extracted words**. Earlier partial Book One readings are preserved in [their original record](science-of-logic-reading-coverage.json), not counted twice as newly completed pages.

**Complete contiguous section:** The Idea, §§1631–1817, across eight HTML pages: overview and embedded text; living individual, life process and genus; rational psychology; analytical science; division; theorem; Good; Absolute Idea. Separately linked texts were read, not merely the overview page.

**Still incomplete:** substantial parts of Being, Essence, and the preceding Subjectivity/Objectivity divisions of the Concept; front matter; the extended mathematical remarks. German and alternative English translations have not been collated. Downloading the remaining text has not been counted as reading it.
